First aid A–Z

Plain-language, step-by-step first aid for the emergencies bystanders face most — CPR, choking, stroke, bleeding, burns and more. Read now, then get certified.

❤

CPR: How to Do Hands-Only CPR on an Adult

Cardiac arrest steps: call, push hard and fast, use an AED.

🫁

Choking: What to Do for Adults and Children

Back blows, abdominal thrusts and when to start CPR.

🧠

Stroke Signs: The FAST Test and What to Do

Face, Arm, Speech, Time — and why minutes matter.

💔

Heart Attack: Symptoms and First Aid

Chest pain, call fast, rest, and be ready for CPR.

🩸

Severe Bleeding: How to Stop Life-Threatening Blood Loss

Direct pressure, packing and tourniquets.

🔥

Burns and Scalds: Cool, Cover, Call

Cool running water for 20 minutes, cover loosely.

⚡

Seizures: How to Help Someone Having a Seizure

Time it, protect the head, never restrain.

🐝

Anaphylaxis: Severe Allergic Reaction First Aid

Use the adrenaline auto-injector and call immediately.

🛌

Unconscious but Breathing: The Recovery Position

Keep the airway open while you wait for help.

☀

Heat Exhaustion and Heatstroke

Cool aggressively — heatstroke is an emergency.

❄

Hypothermia and Frostbite

Rewarm gently, handle carefully.

☠

Poisoning and Overdose: First Steps

Call poison control or emergency services — don't induce vomiting.

🌊

Drowning: Rescue Safely and Start Breaths

Reach or throw, don't go. Breaths matter.

🦴

Broken Bones, Sprains and Head Injuries

Support, immobilise, and know the red flags.

🩺

Diabetic Emergencies: Low Blood Sugar

Fast sugar if they can swallow; call if they can't.

Reading is not training. A 3–4 hour certified course gives you hands-on practice with manikins and AEDs. Find a course near you.
