Do 112 and 911 work in {{ c.c | escape }}?

  • 112: {% if c.m112 %}Yes — 112 connects from mobile phones.{% else %}Not confirmed — use the local numbers above.{% endif %}
  • 911: {% if c.m911 %}Yes — 911 connects or redirects.{% else %}Not confirmed — do not rely on 911 here.{% endif %}

On GSM mobile networks, 112 can often be dialled even without a SIM card or with the keypad locked, and is routed to the local service where supported. Behaviour varies by network and handset, so memorise the local number before you travel.

What to say when you call

  1. Where you are — address, landmark, or GPS coordinates from your phone's map app.
  2. What happened and how many people are hurt.
  3. Your phone number in case the call drops.
  4. Stay on the line and follow instructions. Don't hang up until told to.

If you don't speak the local language, say “ambulance”, “police” or “fire” clearly, then your location; many centres can bring in an interpreter. Carry our emergency phrase card.
