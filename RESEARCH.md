# 91112.com — Research Summary (October 2026)

## 1. What "91112" is
Read as **9-1-1 · 1-1-2** — the two sequences overlap on a shared "1". 911 and 112 are the two universal emergency numbers the ITU recommends to countries choosing one.
- **911**: North America since 1968; adopted by Mexico, Philippines, Saudi Arabia, Argentina and others.
- **112**: single European emergency number since 1991; part of the GSM standard, so many handsets route it to local services worldwide. India unified its emergency lines under 112.
- 91112 is **not** an emergency number anywhere. Search found no notable brand built on "91112"; the value is purely the 911+112 association.

## 3. Chosen concept
**91112 — World Emergency & Safety Hub**: every country's emergency numbers + first aid + preparedness tools, monetised by AdSense (high-CPC adjacent verticals: medical alert, home security, insurance, training), lead generation, sponsorship and donations.

Why this beats alternatives (numerology, generic "number" site, ZIP data): the domain literally *is* the two global emergency numbers → exact-intent search traffic ("emergency number in Thailand", "does 112 work in USA", "CPR steps"), 193 programmatic country pages, evergreen YMYL content and high-value lead categories.
