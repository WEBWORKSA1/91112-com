Home Fire Safety and Escape Planning

Home fire safety: install and test smoke alarms, plan two exits from every room, practise your escape and know what to do if your clothes catch fire.

Emergency? Call 112 now. Dispatchers will guide you through these steps on the phone.

A house fire can become deadly in under three minutes. Smoke, not flames, causes most fire deaths — and it often happens while people sleep.

  1. Install smoke alarms on every level and outside sleeping areas; test monthly and replace units every 10 years.
  2. Plan two ways out of every room and agree an outside meeting point.
  3. Practise your escape at night with children twice a year.
  4. Close doors at night — a closed door slows fire and smoke dramatically.
  5. In a fire: get out, stay out, then call your emergency number. Feel doors with the back of your hand before opening; crawl low under smoke.
  6. Clothes on fire: stop, drop, cover your face and roll.
  7. Kitchen fires: never use water on burning oil — turn off the heat and cover the pan with a lid or fire blanket.
