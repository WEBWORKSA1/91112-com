Earthquake Safety: Before, During and After

Earthquake preparedness and survival: secure your home, drop-cover-hold on during shaking, and stay safe from aftershocks, gas leaks and tsunamis.

Emergency? Call 112 now. Dispatchers will guide you through these steps on the phone.

Before

  • Secure tall furniture, TVs and water heaters to walls; store heavy items low.
  • Identify safe spots in each room — under a sturdy table or against an interior wall.
  • Know how to shut off gas, water and electricity.
  • Keep shoes and a torch by the bed.

During shaking

  1. Drop to your hands and knees before the shaking knocks you down.
  2. Cover your head and neck under a sturdy table or desk; if none, crouch against an interior wall away from windows.
  3. Hold on until the shaking stops.

Do not run outside during shaking — falling glass and masonry cause many injuries. In bed, stay there and cover your head with a pillow. Outdoors, move away from buildings, trees and power lines. Driving, pull over away from bridges and overpasses and stay inside.

After

  • Expect aftershocks; drop, cover and hold on each time.
  • Check for injuries and give first aid. Smell gas? Leave and report it; don't use switches or flames.
  • Near the coast: if shaking was strong or long, move to high ground immediately — don't wait for an official tsunami warning.
  • Text rather than call to keep lines free.
