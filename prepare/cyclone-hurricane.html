Hurricane, Cyclone and Typhoon Preparedness

Get ready for hurricanes, cyclones and typhoons: know your evacuation zone, secure your home, shelter safely and stay away from storm surge and floodwater.

Emergency? Call 112 now. Dispatchers will guide you through these steps on the phone.

The same storm is called a hurricane in the Atlantic and Northeast Pacific, a typhoon in the Northwest Pacific and a cyclone in the South Pacific and Indian Ocean. The deadliest hazards are storm surge and inland flooding, not wind.

  • Know your evacuation zone and leave when ordered — storm surge can rise several metres in minutes.
  • Secure the house: shutters or boarded windows, tie down or bring in outdoor items, trim trees.
  • Stock up on water, food, medication, fuel and cash for a week; charge all devices.
  • Shelter in a small interior room on the lowest level not at risk of flooding.
  • The calm eye passes — the wind will return from the opposite direction. Stay inside until officials give the all-clear.
  • Generators must run outdoors, far from windows, to prevent carbon monoxide poisoning.
