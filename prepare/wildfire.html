Wildfire and Bushfire Readiness

Prepare for wildfires and bushfires: create defensible space, plan to leave early, protect yourself from smoke and know what to do if trapped.

Emergency? Call 112 now. Dispatchers will guide you through these steps on the phone.

  • Create defensible space: clear dry vegetation, gutters and firewood within 10 m (30 ft) of your home.
  • Plan to leave early. Most deaths occur during late evacuations. Know two routes out and leave on official advice — or earlier.
  • Pack the car in advance during high-fire-danger days with go-bag, documents, medications and pets.
  • Protect against smoke: stay indoors with windows shut, run air purifiers, and use well-fitting N95/P2 masks outdoors.
  • If trapped: call your emergency number, shelter in a building away from vegetation, close doors and windows, and stay low under smoke. In a car, park away from fuel, keep windows up, and cover yourself with a wool blanket.
