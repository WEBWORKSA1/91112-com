Travel Safety: Emergency Numbers and Help Abroad

Stay safe abroad: learn local emergency numbers, register with your embassy, buy medical cover and carry a printed emergency card.

Emergency? Call 112 now. Dispatchers will guide you through these steps on the phone.

  1. Learn the local emergency numbers for every country on your itinerary — they differ widely. Build a printable list with our travel emergency card.
  2. Know about 112 and 911. On most GSM mobile networks, 112 is routed to the local emergency service, and many countries also redirect 911 — but not everywhere. Always check.
  3. Register your trip with your government's traveller service and read its advice for each destination.
  4. Buy travel medical insurance with medical evacuation cover; emergency treatment abroad can cost tens of thousands.
  5. Save your embassy's address and phone number and carry copies of your passport separately from the original.
  6. Learn a few phrases — “call an ambulance”, “I need help”, your allergy — or carry our phrase card.
  7. Share your itinerary and location with someone at home.
