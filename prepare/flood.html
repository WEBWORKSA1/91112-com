Flood Safety: How to Prepare and Survive Floods

Flood preparedness and safety: know your risk, prepare your home, evacuate early and never walk or drive through floodwater.

Emergency? Call 112 now. Dispatchers will guide you through these steps on the phone.

Floods are the most common natural disaster worldwide. Most flood deaths happen in vehicles — just 30 cm (1 ft) of moving water can float a car.

Before

  • Check whether your home is in a flood zone and sign up for local warnings.
  • Move valuables and electrical items upstairs; keep sandbags or flood barriers if you're at risk.
  • Review insurance — standard home policies often exclude flood.

During

  • Evacuate when told — early, while routes are open.
  • Never walk, swim or drive through floodwater. It may be deeper, faster and more contaminated than it looks, and can hide open drains and debris.
  • If trapped, go to the highest level of the building (not a closed attic) and signal for help.
  • Switch off power at the mains if safe to do so.

After

  • Return only when authorities say it's safe.
  • Wear gloves and boots; floodwater carries sewage and chemicals.
  • Photograph damage for insurance before cleaning; throw away food that touched floodwater.
