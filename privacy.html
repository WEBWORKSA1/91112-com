Privacy & cookie policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

This policy explains what information 91112.com (“we”) collects and how we use it.

Information you give us

When you submit a form (contact, quote request, donation pledge, contest entry, job application, newsletter, correction) we receive the details you enter. Form submissions are relayed to our inbox through a third-party form-processing service (FormSubmit). We use this information only to respond, to fulfil your request, and — for quote requests where you consent — to share your request with up to three matched providers.

Information stored on your device

Our tools (kit builder, ICE card, travel card, chosen country, theme) save your entries in your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device; we cannot see it. Clear it any time via your browser settings.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. Learn how Google uses data at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message; choosing “Essential only” requests non-personalised ads.

Embedded content

Videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode and only load when you click play. YouTube's privacy policy then applies.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with providers you ask to be matched with, with service providers that help us operate (form processing, hosting), or when required by law.

Retention & your rights

We keep submissions only as long as needed for the purpose. Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA, India's DPDP Act), you may request access, correction or deletion of your data, or object to processing. Contact us via the contact page.

Children

The site is for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or 16 in the EEA); contest entries for minors must be submitted by a parent, guardian or teacher.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses for security purposes.
