Medical disclaimer

Last updated October 22, 2026

If someone is seriously ill or injured, call your local emergency number now.

All first-aid, health and safety content on 91112.com is for general educational purposes. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, or for hands-on training from a certified first-aid provider.

Guidelines differ between countries and organisations and are revised periodically. Always follow the instructions of emergency dispatchers, your local health authority and qualified healthcare professionals. Never delay seeking medical care because of something you read here.

Use of tools such as the CPR metronome or stroke timer is at your own risk; they are aids, not medical devices.

Emergency numbers are compiled from public sources and may change. Verify before travelling.
