Official rules (summary)

  • No purchase or donation necessary. Void where prohibited.
  • Quarterly rounds close on the last day of March, June, September and December, 23:59 UTC.
  • Judged on usefulness (40%), accuracy (30%), creativity (30%) by the 91112 editorial team.
  • Entries must be original, safe to perform and must not show real emergencies or identifiable patients.
  • Minors may only participate via a parent, guardian or teacher; we never publish a minor's full name.
  • Winners are notified by email and must respond within 14 days. Prizes are non-transferable; cash equivalents at our discretion. Taxes are the winner's responsibility.
  • By entering you grant 91112.com a non-exclusive licence to display your entry with credit.
