How prepared are you?

Ten quick questions. Get a readiness score out of 100 and a prioritised list of gaps to close.

1. Does everyone in your home know the emergency number and how to call it from a locked phone?
2. Do you have at least 3 days of water (about 4 L per person per day)?
3. Do you have 3 days of non-perishable food?
4. Do you have a stocked first-aid kit you've checked in the last year?
5. Has anyone in your household done a CPR / first-aid course in the last 2 years?
6. Do you have working smoke alarms on every level, tested this month?
7. Have you agreed a family meeting place and an out-of-area contact?
8. Do you have copies of key documents stored safely (paper + encrypted digital)?
9. Do you have a go-bag ready to grab in under 5 minutes?
10. Do you know your local hazards (flood, quake, fire, storm) and how you'll be warned?
