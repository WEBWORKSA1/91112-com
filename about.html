About 91112

One hub for the world's emergency numbers and the first minutes of help.

Our mission

When something goes wrong, people lose precious time searching for the right number or wondering what to do. 91112.com exists to close that gap: the correct emergency number for wherever you are, and clear, trustworthy steps for the first minutes before professionals arrive.

Editorial policy

  • Sources first. Emergency numbers are compiled from public listings and national authorities and re-checked periodically; each country page shows when it was last checked.
  • Guideline-aligned. First-aid guides summarise mainstream public guidance from resuscitation councils and Red Cross / Red Crescent manuals, in our own words.
  • Medical review. We are recruiting licensed clinicians to review every guide; reviewed pages will carry the reviewer's name and credentials.
  • Corrections. Anyone can report an error; safety-critical corrections are prioritised.
  • Independence. Advertising and sponsorship never influence safety content. Sponsored items are labelled.

How we're funded

Display advertising (Google AdSense), clearly disclosed referral partnerships for safety products and services, sponsorships and voluntary support from readers.

What we are not

We are not an emergency service, a medical provider, a government body, or affiliated with any emergency-number authority. See our medical disclaimer and trademark notice.
